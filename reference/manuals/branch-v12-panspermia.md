Panspermia Variant -

V12. Panspermia

- **V12a.** Panspermia number of players. 2 or 4 competitive. Select 2 species from the list below and use their home world and low orbit. Select a Crew Card/color for them as well. The game plays best if there are 2 players per species.
- **•** Earthlings
- **•** Martians
- **•** Venusians
- **•** Sirens
- **•** Oceanians
- **•** Tholians
- **•** Nuclei
- **V12i.** Earth. If there are no Earthlings in the game, use the Earth overlay (V8b).
- **V12f.** Oceanian Crew is mass 2 (large swimmers) and may land on Sites adjacent to a geyser without a required net thrust.
- **V12h.** Nuclei Crew and Colonists are Mass 0. Size Rolls for the Nuclei Crew and Colonists are automatically successful for Synodic Comets. Due to low gravity, Nuclei can factory refuel 7 steps of water in Home Orbit as if a factory were there, however they cannot convert FT or fuel steps into Aqua.
- **V9b.** V12i. Busted. All Sites of a home world start Busted to indicate that they start inhabited. These Busted sites cannot be re-prospected using special Abilities and Glory chits cannot be picked up there by their inhabitants. For Earthlings, this is Luna.
For the Venusians, this is aerostat-xity. For the Martians, Mars starts Busted and you cannot get a glory chit from any of its Sites or moonlets.

- **V8c.** Mars Moonlets Treaty. If starting on Mars, glory is unavailable on Phobos or Deimos, and Bernals cannot be anchored there.
- **V12g.** Saturn Moonlets Treaty. If starting on Titan, glory is unavailable on Hyperion, and Bernals cannot be anchored there.
- **V12h.** Nuclei Boosts. All boost costs are halved (rounded up) when boosting to Bee-Zed.
- **•** V12h. Nuclei Orbit Refueling. Due to low gravity, Nuclei can factory refuel 7 steps of water in Home Orbit as if a factory were there, however they cannot convert FT or fuel steps into Aqua.
- **V12g.** Tholian Crew & Colonists. When prospecting, Tholian’s Crew and Colonists make a “hydration roll” instead of a Size Roll, which is successful if they roll less than or equal to the Site’s Hydration.
- **V12h.** Nuclei Crew & Colonists. Size Rolls for the Nuclei Crew and Colonists are automatically successful for Synodic Comets.
- **V8c.** Martian/Venusian Home Bernals: Home Bernals may be Anchored on (Venus) the 4 spaces indicated on the Venus overlay card, or (Mars) Sol-Mars Lagrange points (L1, L2, L5) or the Lagrange Burn between the martian moons. Note that there are no Van Allen Belts (VAB) around Venus/Mars, so ignore any special rules associated with them. Note that Tourist Cycler still grants its ability. There is no moon treaty (2Ba), so any player may prospect Luna.
- **V12f.** Oceanians Home Bernals. Possible home orbits are the L2/L1 jovian spaces. Cycler allows safe passage through jovian Radiation Belts.
- **V12g.** Tholian Home Bernals. Possible orbits are L1/L5 (except the innermost and outermost L1) saturnian spaces. Cycler allows safe passage through all Radiation Belts in the Saturn system as well as rings.
- **V12h.** Nuclei Home Bernals. Possible orbits are the first 6 burns from comet Bee-Zed. Cycler works for Jovian flyby Radiation Belts.
- **V12f.** Promotion Colony symbols for the Oceanians are all Submarine and Aerostat, replacing the original dome icons. They only work in the Neptune Zone plus all Aerostats. For dirtside hydration count only Hydration on Submarine Sites, however add 2. Home Orbit Hydration remains at 6.
- **V12g.** Promotion Colonies icons for the Tholian are Atmospheric, replacing the original dome icons. Triton and Pluto also count as having trace atmospheres. Saturn and Mars labs work only if their elevator is built first.
V8c. Mars Space Elevators. If Martians are one of the player Factions, the Space Elevator depicted on the map at Mars can be built only by anchoring the GEO Elevator Bernal (2B4i) to any Home Orbit in Mars (L1, L2, L5, or the Lagrange Burn between the martian moons).

- **V8c.** Footfall and New Venus Futures. These work as written, except the targets are switched: New Venus is now New Earth, and Footfall stomps Venus/Mars.
- **V9e.** Panspermia Victory Conditions. Earthlings score normally per M2. Sirens
, Oceanians, Tholians and Nuclei
