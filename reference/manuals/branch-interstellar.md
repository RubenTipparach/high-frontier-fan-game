Interstellar

- **•** Exodus Cargo at launch can only include 1 TW thruster and 1 or more augmented Colonists. The card holders must allow their cards to be used. The TW thruster must be the one present with the highest base thrust (B2d) plus its supports. The owner of this thruster receives the propulsion medal (4A2o).
If playing the combined game (4E5c), a Promoted Freighter and a Promoted Bernal may also be included at launch.
