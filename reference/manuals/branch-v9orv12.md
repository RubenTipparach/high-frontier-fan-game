# HF4 manual - V9orV12

Auto-extracted from the branching-manuals LyX source. Approximate conversion; see `hf4-branching-manual.md` for full in-order context.

V9 or V12

- **V9c.** Technology Trade. If at the end of your turn one of your Crew or Colonists is colocated with a Crew or Colonist of a different Faction, you may take into your Hand one card from the top of a patent deck of that faction.
- **V8c.** Ersatz LEO. Each ET faction has a space that act as LEO for all purposes: Aqua storage (C5), Crew Decommission location (E7), free market sales (I3), the location for boosted cards (I4), and pad explosions/space debris (K2c). But without the Van Allen Belts, there is no solar flare protection.
- **•** Venusians: Low Venus Orbit (LVO).
- **•** Martians: Low Venus Orbit (LMO). You cannot pick up glory on Mars, Phobos and Deimos.
- **•** Sirens: Cordelia. You cannot pick up glory on Cordelia or Uranus Aerostat.
- **•** Oceanians: Europa Radiation Belt
- **•** Tholian: Low Titan Orbit (LTO)
- **•** Nuclei: Bee-Zed. This asteroid is not a Synodic Comet in this variant. Use its red space as a low orbit. All boost costs are halved (rounded up).
- **V9c.** Cordelia. You cannot pick up glory (L) on Cordelia or Uranus Aerostat.
- **V9c.** Diamonds Aren’t Forever. Sirenian Crew and Colonists from the Siren queue (hereafter called “SIRENS”) are considered rad-hard 0.
- **V9c.** Lone Species. If you are the only player in your species, so that you alone have access to the patent deck of your species, you do not perform auctions. Instead, take the top card of a patent deck for your Operation, including bonus supports (I2g). This costs a number of Aquas equal to the number of cards taken. The academia hand limit still applies. With the Marketeer faction privilege, during research auctions you are allowed to buy 3 cards for 2 aqua.
- **V9c.** Sirenian Home Bernals. The possible Home Orbits are indicated on the map, and any Bernal can go to any Home Orbit. Their dirtside hydration is not six, and instead depends upon the Hydration of the moons they are adjacent to. The Cycler Bernal allows safe passage through the “μ dust ring” Radiation Belt. Uranus Elevator can be built only by anchoring the GEO Elevator or Lofstrom Loop Bernal to any Home Orbit (2B4i).
- **V9c.** Sirenian Promotion Colonies. Regardless of its dome icon, Siren cards can only be promoted at push colonies (2A3a) or promoted and anchored Bernals (2A3c) (representing their need to enter into the inner solar system to meet their demands for solar energy).
- **V9c.** Diamonds Aren’t Forever. If a Stack with Sirenian Crew or Colonists suffers a Glitch, nothing happens if the Stack is on a Site, and the Sirens are Decommissioned if the Stack is in space.
- **V9c.** First Contact. When 2 factions meet for the first time, at the end of a Turn give the player whose Turn it is one of the heroism chits (C7).
