### 1A7. Nanofacture

Your Anchored Bernal can produce its own Mobile Factory. If you have a promoted Freighter, you may create this Mobile Factory (small cube) by performing a nanofacture operation at an Anchored Bernal (but not a Home Bernal). This Decommissions an operational robonaut, refinery, and their supports (including radiators), and places a Mobile Factory cube at the Bernal.
