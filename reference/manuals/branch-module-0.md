## O. Module 0 - POLITICS

In this Module, Factions elect representatives from their population as delegates into 7 different Ideologies, each with its own Law.

- **a.** The Assembly is the hexagonal area on the placard where Factions use elected delegates to manipulate policy and gain advantages in the political arena through Laws.
- **b.** Ideologies include the freedom, honor, unity, authority, equality, individuality, and (in the center) centrist areas of the assembly.
- **c.** Economic vs. Political Pull. You gain delegate cubes by fundraising or building Colonies. You are limited to 7 cubes, which can be used either as Factories or as delegates. Thus, the more you use for Factories means the fewer you can use for delegates. If you run out of cubes for delegates, you may remove a Factory per I7f and vice versa.
- **d.** Active Law. A gold star on the assembly indicates which Law is active.
- **e.** Anarchy. Delegates can be killed by the anarchy event (K2e).
There are two placards: Sol Sunspot Cycle and Sol political assembly.

#### O6. 12-Year Legacy

Whenever a seniority disk is removed, the 1st Player adds it to an Ideology of their choice in the circular spot. This will count as a legacy vote only during the final vote and has no other purpose. They cannot be moved or removed.

- **a.** Final Vote. Placing the final seniority disk into the assembly ends the game. The final 1st Player performs a vote tally (O3a), counting both delegates and legacy votes. The final Active Law affects scoring per M2.
- **b.** Term Limits Baton Pass. Except during martial law (O5d), and the game has not ended per the previous bullet, the 1st Player must then assign another player to be the 1st Player.

### O4. Lobby

As a once-per-turn free action, pay 1 Aqua and Discard 1 of your delegates in an inactive Ideology to use its Law throughout your Turn.

- **a.** Centrist Law (O5g) is exceptional as it may be lobbied only during the pad explosion/space debris event (K2c).
- **O5c.** If the UN General Assembly (unity) law is active: Every other Ideology with 2+ delegates also has its Law activated (O3b), but no player may lobby any Laws.

### O3. Law Activation

After an Operation, free action, or Ability that places, removes, or moves a delegate is finished,always perform a vote tally then activation:

- **a.** Vote Tally. Check if any Ideologies have equal to or more delegates than the Active Law (the active player chooses if tied). If so, the active player must move the Active Law into an Ideology with the most delegates. If more than one Ideology is tied for the most delegates, the Active Law may be moved (or stay) at any of them. If there are no delegates, move the Active Law into Centrist.
- **b.** Activation. Immediately following the vote tally that moves the Active Law into a new Ideology, the Law associated with that Ideology becomes active and may be used by any Faction on their Turn.
- **O5c.** If UN General Assembly (unity) law is active, every other Ideology with 2+ delegates also has its Law activated (O3b), but no player may lobby any Laws.
- **c.** Delegates. For each exomigration, you may gain a delegate of your color in the Ideology indicated on the new Colonist card (O2c), and then perform a vote tally (O3a). Delegates are subject to cube availability (I7f). Emancipated Robots provide Centrist delegates.
