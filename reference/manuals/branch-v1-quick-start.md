### V1. Quick Start Variant.

When playing the 60-year game or longer, or if playing with more than 3 players, the Quick Start Variant is recommended.

- **V1c.** Special Rules.
- **•** 1st Solar Cycle. For the first 12 years, no operations, actions, or events are undertaken. The only option available to a player in her player turn is to take the top card from any patent deck into her Hand. This selected card comes without bonus supports (I2g). The academia hand limit (I2a) is not in force during this process. In the last year, the sunspot cube is not moved (it should therefore be back on the start slot).
- **•** Bonus Round. When each player has obtained 12 cards, a bonus round applies. Each player may sell as many cards as she wishes in any order she chooses for one Aqua each, placing each on the bottom of the relevant patent deck as it is sold. The bonus round is conducted in normal player order. The sunspot cube is not moved.
